## Classification

- Flyer: normally 1–2 pages; may extend to 4 pages when approved.
- Brochure: normally 8–10 pages.
- White paper/case study: separate resource template; not treated as a product
  brochure.

## Inputs needed for each document

- Editable source file, when available.
- Approved product name and short description.
- Current specifications table.
- Approved images in original resolution.
- Required legal, compliance and certification wording.
- Contact email, phone number and download filename.
- Reviewer and approval date.
